---
title: "Can you learn the ropes?"
description: "A historical game in which players crew a British merchant vessel during the Age of Sail, navigating weather, cargo, and the hazards of a world at war."
---

Enter the world of the British merchant navy. Trade ships cross oceans with cargo from silk to spice, carrying news, cultures, languages, goods, and people. Dangers abound from nasty weather to hard work to fraught social encounters. Learn to navigate the global community of the sea by taking up a trade, visiting port, and trying to make your way in the age of sail.
