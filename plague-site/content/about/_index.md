## Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media

RRCHNM was founded in 1994 with a mission to democratize access to history. From the beginning, K-12 education has been a particular focus of the center which as developed a wide variety of free, high-quality educational resources backed by historical scholarship. These resources range from the *Who Built America?* CD-ROM series of the 1990s to our current U.S. history resource hub, [TeachingHistory.org](https://teachinghistory.org), and world history resource hub, [World History Commons](https://worldhistorycommons.org). Even [more educational resources](https://rrchnm.org/our-work/) can be found through our main website. These sites not only feature historical content, but also model how historians approach evidence and think about the past.

## George Mason University

GMU is a public R-1 university located in the Commonwealth of Virginia. Its primary campus, where RRCHNM is located, is in Fairfax, Virginia.
